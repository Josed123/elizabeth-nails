BEFORE YOUR VISIT

Booking Policies

A few important details to help make your experience smooth and enjoyable.

Before Your Appointment
  • Please arrive with bare, natural nails unless a fill or soak-off has been booked in advance.
  • Please include an inspiration photo if you have a specific design in mind.
  • Please do not bring additional guests to your appointment.
Nail Condition & Existing Enhancements
  • Existing enhancements applied by another nail technician cannot be filled.
  • Services are not offered on severely bitten nails.
Appointment Requests & Required Information
  • A clear photo of your hands and current nails must be included with your appointment request.
  • Please describe any existing products on your nails, such as builder gel, acrylic or gel polish.
  • Requests are considered incomplete until the required photo and nail information are received.
  • Appointment requests are not confirmed until Elizabeth reviews and responds to them.
Arrival & Cancellation Policy
  • Please arrive on time for your scheduled appointment.
  • A 10-minute grace period is allowed. Arrivals beyond this period may result in cancellation.
Payment Methods
  • Remaining balances may be paid with cash or Zelle.
  • Apple Pay, credit cards and debit cards are accepted with an additional $2 processing fee.
  • Cash App is also accepted with an additional $2 processing fee.
Repairs & After-Service Support
  • If you experience lifting, a broken nail or missing nail art gems within 48 hours of your appointment, please contact Elizabeth Nails.
  • Eligible repairs within this period will be provided free of charge.
